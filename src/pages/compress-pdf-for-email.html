The target size is set to 10 MB, which fits even strict corporate mail servers; change it to match your provider. The work happens in your browser, so a confidential contract never touches a third-party server on its way to your outbox.

Email attachment limits in practice

Gmail allows 25MB per message, Outlook.com 20MB, Yahoo 25MB, iCloud 20MB. and corporate mail servers are often far stricter, commonly 10MB and sometimes 5MB. The limit applies to the whole message after encoding, which inflates attachments by about a third, so a "25MB" limit really fits roughly an 18MB file. When in doubt, aim well below the ceiling.

Better than a download link

Cloud links expire, need permissions, and get quarantined by security filters. A compressed attachment simply arrives. The Medium level often cuts a scanned contract or slide deck by 60 to 90%, which turns most too-big files into comfortable attachments while keeping them readable and printable.

For recurring email workflows

If you regularly send batches of documents, compress them all at once. each file processes independently in your browser. Pair with the Merge PDF tool to combine several attachments into one tidy file before compressing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should an email attachment be?

Under 10MB is safe for virtually every recipient, including strict corporate servers. Under 5MB is courteous for recipients on mobile connections.

Why does my 24MB PDF bounce with a 25MB limit?

Email encodes attachments in a format that adds roughly 33% overhead, so a 24MB file becomes a 32MB message. Compress the PDF to about 18MB or less to fit a 25MB limit.

Is it safe to compress confidential documents here?

Yes. this is the point of BentoPDF. The file is processed by WebAssembly inside your browser and is never uploaded, so the only copies are on your device and in the email you choose to send.
