To get a PDF under 200KB, add the file below and click Compress PDF. The target size is already set to 200 KB, so Hyper Compress finds the best quality that fits. The compression happens locally in your browser, with no upload, no signup, and no file limit.

When 200KB is the target

A 200KB ceiling shows up on bank KYC uploads, scholarship and exam registration portals, and many national e-government services. It is a friendlier target than 100KB: most everyday documents reach it at the Medium level with no visible quality loss.

Getting a stubborn file under 200KB

If a scanned document stays above 200KB, the result tells you how close it got. For a scan with no text layer, Rasterize pages under Custom Settings can go smaller. Multi-page scans benefit from removing blank or irrelevant pages first: every scanned page you delete is often 100KB saved before compression even starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What quality should I expect at 200KB?

Typed documents look identical. Scans keep readable text; photos inside the PDF will look slightly softer but remain clear on screen.

The portal wants under 200KB but my file is 10MB. possible?

Usually yes, if most of the weight is images. With the 200 KB target set, Hyper Compress keeps lowering image quality and resolution until the file fits, and tells you if it can't get there.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Every BentoPDF tool is free with no account, and because the work happens in your browser there are no server costs to pass on to you.
